The game

The Duelo Arcano game works offline: your progress and settings stay only on your device. The app connects to the internet only to check for updates on the Play Store and for the contact features described below. There are no accounts, ads or in-app purchases.

Messages inside the game

If you use Message the developer or Bug report inside the game, the app connects to our server and we store: the text you write (bug reports also include the screen, your level in the adventure, the device model and the Android version), a random installation identifier created by the server, your IP address and dates. A copy is sent to the studio mailboxes so we can read and reply. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS).

Site contact form

If you use the site contact form, we store your name (optional), email, message, IP address and date. We use the email only to reply.

Current retention

Current retention: chat messages are deleted from the server after about 90 days; the IP address on installation records is deleted after about 90 days; installations with no contact for about 180 days are deleted together with their messages; site form messages are deleted after about 90 days; emailed copies are deleted after 180 days by a manual studio routine. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time through the contact form.

Your data

For requests about your data (access, correction or deletion), use the contact form. You can complain to the CNPD, the Portuguese data protection authority.
