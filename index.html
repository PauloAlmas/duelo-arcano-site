Help shape the game

Become a beta tester.

Duelo Arcano is in closed testing on Google Play. Try the game, tell us what works and help us find the bugs.

Use the same Google account for the group, the test page and the Play Store on your phone.

  1. Join the testers group

    First, join Almas Dev Studio Testers on Google Groups.

    Join the group

  2. Opt in on Google Play

    Open the test page and choose "Become a tester".

    Open the test page

  3. Install, play & share feedback

    Install from Google Play after opting in. Stay opted in and keep the game installed for at least 14 consecutive days. Play during the test and send us your feedback.

This is a beta: you may find bugs. Screenshots show the Portuguese interface; an English in-game version is planned.
